# Security policy

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem in Conn3x or any
conn3xorg circuit.

Report it privately through the repository's **Security** tab
(Report a vulnerability), or email **security@conn3x.org**.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the version you tested.
You'll get an acknowledgement within 3 business days, and we'll keep you
posted while we work on a fix. Once a fix ships, we publish an advisory and
credit you, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.
